## Description
A little space for what matters.

Aperio brings thoughtful Bible reading and deeper study together in a calm, carefully designed experience.

READ THE WORD
Explore all 66 books of the Berean Standard Bible, with introductions that set the scene and readable typography you can adjust. Scripture is included for offline reading.

GO DEEPER
Open Study Center from your passage. Explore saved Devotional, Scholarly, and Prophetic commentary, verse breakdowns, Hebrew and Greek word definitions, available cross-references, and context within the biblical timeline. Study content is prepared in advance and included in the app.

FOLLOW A THREAD
Discover short reading journeys that connect passages around rest, wisdom, prayer, mercy, and hope. Motion covers bring each thread to life, while your progress helps you continue at your own pace.

MAKE ROOM FOR PRAYER
Write prayers, record answers, and return to the words that matter. Tap a verse to highlight it, keep a bookmark, or add a personal note without opening Study Center.

LEARN A LITTLE EACH DAY
Daily Scripture and short quizzes connect discovery with context and reflection. Each quiz includes explanations and links back to the passage.

YOUR READING SPACE
Use the floating menu to move between Home, Bible, Prayer, Saved, and Account. Adjust text size, typeface, and motion to make the experience your own.

No account is required. Personal notes, prayers, highlights, and progress are saved on your device. Export a personal backup from Account to keep a copy. This version does not sync with your Aperio website account or other devices.

## Review notes
Aperio Bible 2.0.0 (build 27) is a new native iOS app based on the current Aperio website's design.

1. No sign-in or payment is required. Scripture, book introductions, saved commentary, lexicon data, available cross-references, timeline, reading journeys, quizzes, and prayer tools are bundled for offline use. There are no AI requests when opening a verse or study entry.

2. This build removes the earlier native giving flow associated with the previous 3.1.1 rejection. It contains no donation, subscription, in-app purchase, or paid feature flow. The separate Aperio website has optional giving; that checkout is not part of this native app.

3. Account is a local profile and backup screen, not a sign-in screen. Personal content stays on the device. There is no Apple/Google sign-in, account creation, server account, or cloud sync in this build. Account provides user-initiated backup export and restore. Compatible personal content already stored locally by the earlier app can be imported; this does not retrieve older cloud-only content.

4. To navigate, tap the floating + button. Choose Bible, then a book and chapter. The Study Center pill opens the bottom study sheet. Tap a verse directly to open highlights, notes, and bookmarking. Prayer opens the prayer journal; Saved shows personal verse entries.
