## Name
Aperio Bible

## Subtitle

## Description
A little space for what matters.

Aperio brings thoughtful Bible reading and deeper study together in a calm, carefully designed experience.

READ THE WORD
Explore all 66 books of the Berean Standard Bible, with introductions that set the scene and readable typography you can adjust. Scripture is included for offline reading.

GO DEEPER
Open Study Center alongside your passage. Explore saved Devotional, Scholarly and Prophetic commentary, Hebrew and Greek word definitions, available cross-references, and context within the biblical story.

FOLLOW A THREAD
Discover short reading journeys that connect passages around rest, wisdom, prayer, mercy and hope. Continue through each thread at your own pace.

MAKE ROOM FOR PRAYER
Write prayers, record answers and return to the words that matter. Highlight verses, keep bookmarks and add personal notes as you read.

LEARN A LITTLE EACH DAY
Daily Scripture and short quizzes connect discovery with context and reflection.

This preview stores personal notes, prayers and reading progress on your device. Export a personal backup from Account to keep a copy.

## What's new
A fresh native Aperio experience, inspired by our new website: a graphite, pearl and olive design; an offline Bible and Study Center; motion-covered reading journeys; verse notes and highlights; and a private prayer journal.
