The vision

A free biblical resource, with one purpose.

Aperio exists to help people get closer to God, get encouraged, and grow curious about who He has called them to be.

01

Get closer to God

All 66 books, commentary in three voices, and the original languages one tap away — depth without the seminary degree.

02

Be encouraged

A Scripture every morning, told as a story — verse, devotion, reflection, and prayer. A daily quiz. A prayer journal that remembers what God answered.

03

Discover your calling

Curiosity is holy. Cross references, word studies, and reading journeys built to make you ask, "Who has God called me to be?"
