The vision

A free biblical resource,
with one purpose.

Aperio exists to help people get closer to God, get encouraged, and grow curious about who He has called them to be.

01

Get closer to God

All 66 books, a Study Center with commentary in three voices, and the original languages one tap away — depth without the seminary degree.

02

Be encouraged

A Scripture every morning, told as a story — verse, devotion, reflection, and prayer. A daily quiz. A prayer journal that remembers what God answered.

03

Discover your calling

Curiosity is holy. Cross references, word studies, and reading journeys built to make you lean in and ask, "Who has God called me to be?"

What's inside

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

📖

Its own translation

The New Clavis Translation — an original English rendering of all 66 books, made to be read with its commentary.

🗝️

The Study Center

Commentary in three voices — Devotional, Scholarly, Prophetic — with cross references for every chapter.

🏛️

Original languages

Tap any gold word for Strong's Hebrew and Greek — 14,197 lexicon entries, fully offline.

🌅

Scripture of the Day

Each morning's verse, told as a story you can tap through — and delivered as a private daily notification.

🙏

Prayer journal

A quiet place to bring requests, keep a streak, and mark the moment a prayer is answered.

✈️

Offline & private

The entire Bible, commentary, and lexicon live on your phone. No account needed. Your faith data is never sold.
