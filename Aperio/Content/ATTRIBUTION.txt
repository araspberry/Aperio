Berean Standard Bible text and translation tables
Produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, unfoldingWord,
Bible Aquifer, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee.
https://berean.bible/downloads.htm
https://berean.bible/licensing.htm
The publisher dedicates the Bible texts to the public domain.
Inputs: https://bereanbible.com/bsb.txt and https://bereanbible.com/bsb_tables.tsv
Aperio keeps the verse text unchanged. Study phrases are linked only where an
exact sequential phrase match can be located in that verse. Unmatched table
phrases are omitted, so this is an initial, incomplete alignment import.

Strong's Hebrew and Greek dictionaries
By James Strong. JSON editions copyright 2010 (Hebrew) and 2009 (Greek),
Open Scriptures. CC-BY-SA, as stated in the upstream file headers.
https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs
Full original files and notices are provided in sources/ and in the
hebrew-dictionary-notice.txt and greek-dictionary-notice.txt files.
Aperio's data/hebrew.json and data/greek.json are format conversions from
those editions with dictionary entries unmodified, under the same CC-BY-SA
terms. Dictionary content is distinct from Aperio application code.
The dictionaries provide historical glosses, not definitive contextual
interpretations. Original-language forms and transliterations come from
the BSB tables; dictionary headwords may differ from inflected forms.

Passage cross-references: extracted from the public-domain BSB translation
published tables at https://bereanbible.com/bsb_tables.tsv, linked from
https://berean.bible/downloads.htm. This import includes 2,018 validated,
explicit single-chapter verse references; 15 ambiguous or mismatched links
were omitted. Coverage is 632 chapters. References are distinct from lexical
occurrence matches. The publisher's attachment verse may introduce a passage.

Aperio commentary: new original Genesis 12 and Psalm 23 editorial drafts, with three
perspectives and passage-level sections. This content has not completed
editorial or theological review. Scripture sources are linked within it.
